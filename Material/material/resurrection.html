Lesson 1

The Power of Christ's Resurrection

Key Scripture: 1 Corinthians 15:14-17

"And if Christ has not been raised, our preaching is useless and so is your faith." — 1 Corinthians 15:14 (NIV)
📥 Download Lesson Visual

Lesson Summary

The resurrection of Jesus Christ stands as the foundational pillar of the Christian faith. In 1 Corinthians 15, Paul addresses doubts surrounding the physical resurrection and demonstrates that without the resurrection, faith remains powerless and devoid of hope.

Key Themes for the Week

  • Victory Over Death: Christ’s resurrection guarantees the future hope and eternal life of every believer.
  • Validation of the Gospel: The resurrection serves as divine proof that Christ’s sacrifice on the cross was complete and accepted.
  • Living with Hope: Knowing Christ is alive empowers believers to endure present suffering with confidence.

Discussion Questions

  1. How does the reality of the resurrection impact your daily response to challenges and grief?
  2. Why is the bodily resurrection of Jesus essential to our salvation and future hope?
