Lesson 1 • Quarter 3

The Power of Christ's Resurrection

Memory Text: 1 Corinthians 15:14-17

"And if Christ has not been raised, our preaching is useless and so is your faith." — 1 Corinthians 15:14 (NIV)
🖼️ Download Visual 🎧 Listen Audio (12 min)

Executive Summary

The resurrection of Jesus Christ is not merely an encouraging historical event; it is the fundamental pillar of the Christian hope. In 1 Corinthians 15, the Apostle Paul directly addresses the doubts surrounding the resurrection and lays out the logical consequence: if Christ did not rise, our faith is empty.

Key Takeaways for the Week

  • The Hope Beyond the Grave: Christ's victory over death guarantees the future resurrection of believers.
  • Vindicated Faith: The resurrection serves as God's validation of Christ's sacrifice on the cross.
  • Daily Empowerment: The same power that raised Jesus from the dead is available to believers today to overcome present trials.

Discussion & Study Questions

  1. How does the reality of Christ’s resurrection change the way we handle grief and loss today?
  2. In what ways does living with a "resurrection perspective" impact our daily daily choices and ministry?
