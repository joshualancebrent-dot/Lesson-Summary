Lesson 2

Love-driven Ministry

Key Scripture: 2 Corinthians 2:4

"For I wrote you out of great distress and anguish of heart and with many tears, not to grieve you but to let you know the depth of my love for you." — 2 Corinthians 2:4 (NIV)
📥 Download Lesson Visual

Lesson Summary

Effective Christian ministry is grounded not in obligation, authority, or routine, but in deep, selfless love for others. Paul’s letters to the Corinthians demonstrate that genuine spiritual leadership requires vulnerability, compassion, and a heart dedicated to reconciliation.

Key Themes for the Week

  • Compassion in Correction: True ministry addresses difficulties with tears and love rather than harshness.
  • Selfless Service: Motivated by Christ’s unconditional love, believers serve without seeking personal gain or prestige.
  • Restoration & Forgiveness: Love seeks to heal broken relationships and bring believers closer to God and one another.

Discussion Questions

  1. How can we ensure our service in church and community is driven by love rather than duty?
  2. Why is vulnerability and genuine care essential when helping others through spiritual struggles?
