Lesson 1

The Power of Christ's Resurrection

Key Scripture: 1 Corinthians 15:14-17

"And if Christ has not been raised, our preaching is useless and so is your faith." — 1 Corinthians 15:14 (NIV)
📥 Download Lesson Visual

Lesson Summary

Main Idea:

Christ’s resurrection is the foundation of the gospel, the proof of His victory, and the source of our hope for salvation and eternal life.

Key Themes for the Week

  • ✝️ 1. The Gospel We Preach and Believe The resurrection is at the center of the gospel. Paul reminds the Corinthians that Christ: Died for our sins, Was buried and Rose again on the third day. This was not a new idea created by the apostles. Paul says it happened according to the Scriptures. Teaching point: We don't preach merely a dead Christ who sacrificed Himself. We preach a risen Christ who conquered death. 📖 1 Corinthians 15:3–4
  • 👥 2. Eyewitnessed and Proven Paul then gives evidence that Jesus really rose. After His resurrection, Jesus appeared to: Cephas (Peter),the twelve disciples, more than 500 believers, James, and Paul The point is clear: The resurrection was presented as a witnessed event, not a myth or imaginary experience. The early Christian message was built on the testimony of people who claimed to have actually encountered the risen Christ. 📖 1 Corinthians 15:5–6
    • Teaching point: Our faith rests on the risen Christ, not merely on an inspiring religious idea.
  • 🛡️ 3. Our Faith Depends on the Resurrection This is Paul's strongest argument. He essentially says: If Christ has not been raised, then everything falls apart. If there is no resurrection: our preaching is empty, our faith is useless, our sins remain unforgiven, and Christians have no real hope. That's why the resurrection isn't just one doctrine among many. It is foundational to the Christian faith. 📖 1 Corinthians 15:17 Teaching point: Remove the resurrection, and the foundation of Christianity collapses. But because Christ has risen, our faith has a foundation.

Discussion Questions

  1. How does the reality of the resurrection impact your daily response to challenges and grief?
  2. Why is the bodily resurrection of Jesus essential to our salvation and future hope?
