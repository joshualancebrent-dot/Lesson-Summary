Lesson 8

The Power of Christ's Resurrection

Key Scripture: 1 Corinthians 15:14-17

"And if Christ has not been raised, our preaching is useless and so is your faith." — 1 Corinthians 15:14 (NIV)
📥 Download Lesson Visual

Lesson Summary

Main Idea:

'Christ’s resurrection is the foundation of the gospel, the proof of His victory, and the source of our hope for salvation and eternal life."

Key Themes for the Week

  • ✝️ 1. The Gospel We Preach and Believe The resurrection is at the center of the gospel. Paul reminds the Corinthians that Christ: Died for our sins, Was buried and Rose again on the third day. This was not a new idea created by the apostles. Paul says it happened according to the Scriptures.

    • Teaching point:

  • We don't preach merely a dead Christ who sacrificed Himself. We preach a risen Christ who conquered death. 📖 1 Corinthians 15:3–4
  • 👥 2. Eyewitnessed and Proven Paul then gives evidence that Jesus really rose. After His resurrection, Jesus appeared to: Cephas (Peter),the twelve disciples, more than 500 believers, James, and Paul The point is clear: The resurrection was presented as a witnessed event, not a myth or imaginary experience. The early Christian message was built on the testimony of people who claimed to have actually encountered the risen Christ. 📖 1 Corinthians 15:5–6

    • Teaching point:

    Our faith rests on the risen Christ, not merely on an inspiring religious idea.
  • 🛡️ 3. Our Faith Depends on the Resurrection This is Paul's strongest argument. He essentially says: If Christ has not been raised, then everything falls apart. If there is no resurrection: our preaching is empty, our faith is useless, our sins remain unforgiven, and Christians have no real hope. That's why the resurrection isn't just one doctrine among many. It is foundational to the Christian faith. 📖 1 Corinthians 15:17

    • Teaching point:

    Remove the resurrection, and the foundation of Christianity collapses. But because Christ has risen, our faith has a foundation.
  • 👑 4. Our Living Hope and Future GloryThe resurrection doesn't only explain what happened to Jesus. It tells us what will happen to those who belong to Him. Paul calls Christ: “the firstfruits” This means Christ's resurrection points forward to the resurrection of His people. He conquered sin and death, and those who belong to Him will ultimately be raised to live with Him. 📖 1 Corinthians 15:20

  • Teaching point:

    Jesus' resurrection is the guarantee of our future resurrection. Our story doesn't end at the grave.

🔑 Put the Four Points Together

  • 1. THE GOSPEL

    Christ died and rose again.

    • ⬇️

  • 2. THE EVIDENCE

    His resurrection was witnessed and proclaimed.

    • ⬇️

  • 3. THE FOUNDATION

    Our faith depends upon His resurrection.

    • ⬇️

  • 4. THE HOPE

    Because Christ lives, we will live with Him.

    • Closing Thought

    “The resurrection is not simply something we believe happened in the past. It changes our present and guarantees our future. Because Christ died, we have forgiveness. Because Christ rose, we have victory. Because Christ lives, we have hope. And because He is the firstfruits, we can look forward to the day when we too will be raised.” Risen Savior. Living Hope. Eternal Victory.
