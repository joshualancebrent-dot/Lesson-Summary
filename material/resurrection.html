The Power of Christ's Resurrection

1 Corinthians 15:14-17

"And if Christ has not been raised, our preaching is useless and so is your faith."

— 1 Corinthians 15:14

The resurrection of Jesus Christ stands as the pivotal pillar of Christian faith. Without His triumphant victory over the grave, our faith would be devoid of ultimate hope and eternal promise.

Key Takeaways

  • Living Hope: Christ's resurrection promises our future victory over death.
  • Transformed Lives: The same power that raised Jesus lives within us today.
  • Eternal Victory: Sin and death have lost their sting forever.
