Lesson 11

Stewardship and Mission

Key Scripture: 2 Corinthians 8:9

"For you know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, that though He was rich, yet for your sakes He became poor, that you through His poverty might become rich." — 2 Corinthians 8:9 (NKJV)
📥 Download Lesson Visual

Lesson Summary

Main Idea:

Stewardship and mission are inseparable. God gives so that we may give, reflecting Christ's self-giving love and advancing His mission in the world.

Key Themes for the Week

  • 1. The Example of Jesus

    Though He was rich, Jesus became poor for us. He gave up heaven, lived humbly, and died on the cross. His purpose was that we might become rich in salvation.

    Teaching point: Christ's generosity toward us is the pattern for our generosity toward others. 📖 2 Corinthians 8:9

  • 2. Motivation: Giving From the Heart

    Paul teaches that we should give willingly, not out of compulsion. God loves a cheerful and generous giver, and He supplies all we need and multiplies our giving for His glory.

    Teaching point: "Each one must give as he has decided in his heart, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver." 📖 2 Corinthians 9:7

  • 3. Stewardship for the Saints and the Mission

    Our gifts meet needs and strengthen the church. Giving supports the spread of the gospel — we are co-workers with God in His mission.

    Teaching point: "That they may glorify God for your obedience to the gospel of Christ and for the generosity of your sharing with them and with all." 📖 2 Corinthians 9:13

  • 4. Abundant Grace, Abundant Thanksgiving

    God gives abundantly so we can give abundantly. Our giving results in thanksgiving to God, and all glory belongs to Him who provides.

    Teaching point: "Thanks be to God for His indescribable gift!" 📖 2 Corinthians 9:15

🔑 Key Spiritual Principle

We are stewards of God's grace and resources, called to give generously so others may know Christ.

Closing Thought

God gave everything for us; we give back with grateful hearts to bless others and advance His mission. Empowered by Christ's grace, we manage His gifts faithfully and share them generously to reach the world with His love and truth.

Give with Joy. Serve with Love. Share for His Glory.
